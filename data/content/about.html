About the Cottid Africa Food Basket Platform

An information and data platform on the food Africa grows, eats, trades and imports. Version 33 · 23 November 2026 · landed-cost library 18 November 2026 · statistical database built 2026-11-16.

What it is

The platform brings the published record on 34 foods in 9 food groups into one place for all 54 African countries: what each country produces, consumes, imports and exports, what it is likely to need over the next five to ten years, and what it would take to grow more of its own.

  • Cereals: rice, wheat, maize, barley and malt, sorghum, millet, wheat flour.
  • Roots, tubers and plantains: cassava, yams, potatoes, sweet potatoes, plantains.
  • Pulses, groundnuts and soybeans: pulses, groundnuts, soybeans.
  • Sugar and edible oils: sugar, edible oils.
  • Vegetables: tomatoes, onions, other vegetables.
  • Fruits: bananas, citrus fruit, other fruits.
  • Meat: beef, poultry meat, sheep and goat meat, pork, other meat and offal.
  • Fish, milk and eggs: fish and seafood, milk and dairy, eggs.
  • Coffee, tea and cocoa: coffee, tea, cocoa.

Depth differs by food. The ten traded staples (rice, wheat, maize, barley, sorghum, millet, sugar, edible oils, pulses, wheat flour) also carry landed cost, customs trade by supplier, import values and the buyer, seller and compliance registers. The other foods carry production, consumption, trade volumes, self-sufficiency, projections and plans, and where they are traded, customs trade by supplier and destination for 2024.

It is an information service. It does not quote prices, broker deals or trade commodities.

How we work

  • Every figure is sourced. Each number carries a source ID that resolves on the tab.
  • Estimates are labelled. Cottid Africa rate cards, modelling assumptions and projections are marked as such and kept apart from the published record.
  • Gaps are shown. Where a country or food has no data the page says "data not available" and does not fill the gap.
  • Leads are not presented as facts. Directory entries are marked verified, with the kind of evidence, or "lead to verify".
  • The model is tested on the past and the result is published, including where it adds little. See .
